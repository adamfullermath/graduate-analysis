Exercise 2.

Let \(f \colon [0,1] \rightarrow \bR\) be a continuous function with continuous bounded derivative \(f'\) and let \(C = \{(x,f(x)) \colon x \in [0,1]\}\subseteq \bR^2\) be the graph of \(f\text{.}\) Note that \(f\) is of bounded variation by Exercise II.7.5.6. Let \(\nu\) be the Borel measure on \([0,1]\) induced by \(f\) and let \(|\nu|\) be the measure of total variation of \(\nu.\) Define a map \(F \colon [0,1] \rightarrow C\) by \(F(t)=(t,f(t))\text{,}\) and let \(\mu\) be the Borel measure on \(C\) defined by \(\mu(E) = |\nu|(F^{-1}(E)).\) Extend \(\mu\) to \(\bR^2\) by setting \(\mu(B) = \mu(B\cap C)\) for all Borel \(B \subseteq \bR^2\text{.}\)
