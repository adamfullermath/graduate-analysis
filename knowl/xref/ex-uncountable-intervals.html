Example A.2.9.

An argument similar to that of Proposition A.2.8 can be used to show that each of \((0,1]\text{,}\) \([0,1)\) and \((0,1)\) are uncountable. It follows that any non-empty, non-singleton interval in \(\bR\) is uncountable. For example, if \(a \lt b\) then
\begin{align*} f \colon (0,1) \amp \rightarrow (a,b)\\ x\amp \mapsto (b-a)x+a \end{align*}
is a bijection from \((0,1)\) to \((a,b)\text{,}\) and hence \((0,1)\) and \((a,b)\) have the same cardinality.
