Exercise 2.

Let \(\bT = \{ z \in \bC \colon |z|=1\}\) be the unit circle in \(\bC\text{.}\) Let \(\mu\) be the Haar measure on \(\bT\) as given in Example 2.2.5. Extend \(\mu\) to a Borel measure on \(\bC\) by setting \(\mu(E) = \mu(E\cap \bT)\) for all Borel \(E \subseteq \bC\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Let \(A\) be a closed arc on \(\bT\text{.}\) That is, \(A = \{ e^{i\theta} \colon a \leq \theta \leq a + \alpha\}\) for some \(a\in \bR\) and \(\alpha \gt 0\text{.}\) Show that
\begin{equation*} \diam(A) \leq \mu(A) \leq 4\diam(A). \end{equation*}
🔗
Hint.
Note that \(\mu(A) = \alpha\) is the arc length of \(A\text{,}\) and \(\diam(A) = 2 \sin(\alpha/2)\) is the length of the chord from \(e^{ia}\) to \(e^{i(a+\alpha)}\) when \(\alpha \leq \pi.\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Show that
\begin{equation*} H_1(\bT) \geq \frac{\pi}{2}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Hint.
Show that if \(E \subseteq \bC\) Borel with \(\diam(E)\lt 1\) then there is an arc \(A \subseteq \bC\) such that \(E\cap \bT \subseteq A\text{,}\) and apply Exercise 3.3.2.a. Use this to get a lower bound on \(H_{1,\delta}(\bT)\) when \(\delta\lt 1.\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Show that \(\dim_H(\bT) =1\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
That \(\dim_H(\bT)\geq 1\) follows from Exercise 3.3.2.b. For the reverse inequality, divide \(\bT\) into \(\text{}\) equal length arcs (for large enough \(n\)), and see what this says about \(H_{1,\delta}(\bT)\)
