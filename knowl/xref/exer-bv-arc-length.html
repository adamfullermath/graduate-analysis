Exercise 10.

Let \(f\) be a continuous function on a closed interval \([a,b]\text{.}\) For a partition \(P = \{a=x_0\lt \ldots \lt x_n = b\}\) of \([a,b]\) define \(L(f,P)\) as
\begin{equation*} L(f,P) = \sum_{i=1}^n \sqrt{(x_i - x_{i-1})^2 - (f(x_i)-f(x_{i-1})^2)}, \end{equation*}
and define the arc length \(L\) of \(f\) on \([a,b]\) as
\begin{equation*} L = sup_P L(f,P). \end{equation*}
Show that \(f\) is of bounded variation if and only if \(L\lt \infty\text{.}\)
