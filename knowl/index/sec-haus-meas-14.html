Definition 3.1.8.

Let \(E \subseteq \bR^n\) be a Borel set. If \(p \geq 0\) satisfies
\begin{equation*} p = \inf\{ q \colon H_q(E)=0\} (= \sup \{q \colon H_q(E)=\infty\}) \end{equation*}
we say that \(E\) has Hausdorff dimension \(p\text{.}\) We write this as
\begin{equation*} \dim_H(E) = p. \end{equation*}
