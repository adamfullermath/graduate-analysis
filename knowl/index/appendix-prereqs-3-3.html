The complement of \(A\) in \(B\) is given by
\begin{equation*} B \backslash A = \{b \in B \colon b\notin A\}. \end{equation*}
When it is clear what ambient set \(X\) we are working in we will often refer to \(X\backslash A\) as simply the complement of \(A\text{.}\) We will also write \(X\backslash A\) as \(A^c\text{.}\) For example, we will often be implicitly be working with subsets of \(\mathbb{R}\text{.}\) Thus, by “the complement of \([0,1]\)” we will mean
\begin{equation*} \mathbb{R}\backslash [0,1] = (-\infty, 0) \cup (1,\infty). \end{equation*}
