Let \(H\) be the Heaviside function on \(\mathbb{R}\text{.}\) That is
\begin{equation*} H(x) = \begin{cases} 0 & x<0 \\ 1 & x \geq 0.\end{cases} \end{equation*}
The Heaviside function is the cumulative distribution function for the Dirac measure at \(0\text{,}\) \(\delta_0\) (see Exercise 3.4.8). Thus, if \(x_0 \in \mathbb{R}\text{,}\) then the cumulative distribution function for the Dirac measure \(\delta_{x_0}\) is \(H(x-x_0).\)
