Let \(\mathcal{F}\) be a collection of subsets of \(X\text{.}\) The following two identities are called De Morgan’s Identities.
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} X \backslash \left[\bigcup_{F\in \mathcal{F}} F\right] & = \bigcap_{F\in \mathcal{F}}X\backslash F, \\ X \backslash \left[\bigcap_{F\in \mathcal{F}} F\right] & = \bigcup_{F\in \mathcal{F}}X\backslash F. \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
