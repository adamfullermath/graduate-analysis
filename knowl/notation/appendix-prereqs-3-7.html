For a function/mapping \(f \colon E \rightarrow B\) we let
\begin{equation*} f^{-1}(E) = \{a\in A \colon f(a) \in E\}. \end{equation*}
We call \(f^{-1}(E)\) the inverse image of \(E\) under \(f\). Note that the inverse image of a set is defined whether or not the mapping \(f\) is invertible.
